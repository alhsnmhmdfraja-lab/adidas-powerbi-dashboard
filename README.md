Adidas Sales Analytics Dashboard – Power BI

📊 Overview

From Raw Sales Data to Business Insights

How can raw sales data be transformed into a clear and meaningful view of business performance?

In this project, I developed an Adidas Sales Analytics Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI to transform raw sales data into clear business insights.

The data was prepared and transformed using Power Query, while DAX was used to create measures and key performance indicators. The dashboard brings together sales, profit, orders, products, retailers, and geographic performance into a single analytical view.

The analysis helps provide a clearer understanding of sales performance, profitability, product performance, retailer contribution, regional performance, and changes in business activity over time.

📈 Key KPIs

The dashboard uses six key performance indicators to provide a quick overview of the main aspects of business performance:

- Total Orders — Measures the overall number of orders.
- Total Profit — Measures the total profit generated from sales.
- Total Sales — Measures the overall sales value.
- Units Sold — Measures the total quantity of products sold.
- Average Order Value — Measures the average sales value generated per order.
- Profit Margin — Evaluates profitability relative to sales.

Together, these KPIs provide a concise view of sales volume, profitability, order activity, and overall business performance.

📊 Business Analysis

The dashboard focuses on understanding business performance across several important dimensions:

- Product Performance — Understanding which products generate stronger sales and contribute more to overall performance.
- Regional Performance — Comparing sales activity and product demand across different regions.
- Retailer Performance — Evaluating sales and profitability across different retail channels.
- Sales Trends — Identifying changes and patterns in sales performance over time.
- Geographic Profitability — Comparing markets to identify areas with stronger profit performance.
- Performance Comparison — Examining differences between products, retailers, regions, and markets to understand where performance is stronger or weaker.

This approach transforms individual sales records into a structured view of business performance, making the data easier to analyze and interpret.

🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project was not simply to create a dashboard filled with charts and numbers, but to transform Adidas sales data into a clear and structured business analysis.

The project focuses on analyzing sales and profitability patterns, comparing products, retailers, and geographic markets, and transforming raw data into meaningful insights that can support better understanding of business performance and data-driven decision-making.
