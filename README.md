Adidas Sales Analytics Dashboard – Power BI

📊 Overview

From Raw Data to Business Insights

How can raw sales data be transformed into a clear understanding of business performance?

In this project, I developed an Adidas Sales Analytics Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI, starting with data cleaning and transformation using Power Query, followed by building a clear analytical dashboard to understand sales, profit, products, retailers, and geographic performance.

I then used DAX to create measures and calculate key business KPIs, transforming raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

📈 Key KPIs

- Total Orders
- Total Profit
- Total Sales
- Units Sold
- Average Order Value
- Profit Margin

📊 Business Analysis

The dashboard provides a focused analysis of Adidas sales performance across different business dimensions.

1. Sales & Profitability Analysis

Analyzes Sales, Profit, and Profit Margin to understand overall business performance and profitability.

2. Product & Retailer Analysis

Compares product and retailer performance to understand sales contribution and identify stronger-performing areas.

3. Regional & Geographic Analysis

Analyzes performance across Regions, States, and Cities to understand geographic differences in sales and profitability.

4. Sales Trend Analysis

Tracks changes in sales performance over time to identify important patterns and changes in business activity.

🎯 Project Objective

The goal was not simply to create a visually appealing dashboard, but to build a structured business analysis that transforms raw Adidas sales data into clear insights.

The project focuses on understanding sales and profitability, comparing product, retailer, and geographic performance, and identifying patterns that provide a clearer view of overall business performance.
